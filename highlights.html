Highlights

Competitions, science fairs, and certifications

A running trace of the competitions and certificates that shaped me as a student — from early science fairs to university club events. The line connecting them is drawn the way I'd draw a trace on a breadboard.

Jan 2016

14th Bangladesh Mathematical Olympiad — Regional Round

Competed in the regional round of the Bangladesh Mathematical Olympiad, organized by the Bangladesh Mathematical Olympiad Committee, representing Faizur Rahman Ideal Institute.

Mar 2016

Science Fair — Certificate of Participation

Took part in the Science Club's yearly science fair at Faizur Rahman Ideal Institute's Mohammadpur/Malibaag/Banasree campus.

Mar 2018

Science Fair — 3rd Position, Certificate of Excellence

Returned to the same science fair two years later and placed 3rd for the project — earning a Certificate of Excellence instead of participation this time.

Oct 2025

Poster Presentation — World Mental Health Day, NSU

Presented a poster on "Access to services — Mental Health in Catastrophes & Emergencies," in observance of World Mental Health Day at North South University, organized by the Dept. of Public Health, the Counseling & Wellbeing Center, and the NSU Public Health & Sciences Club.

2026

NSUCEC Cyber-Nauts 2026 — Robo Soccer

Took part in the Robo Soccer segment of Cyber-Nauts 2026, organized by the North South University Computer Engineering Club.
